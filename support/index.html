Tabiya Support

Need help with Tabiya? Email tabiya.chess@gmail.com. Tabiya does not require an account, so there is no password or username to include.

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • the App version shown at the bottom of Tabiya’s Settings screen;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the problem; and
  • a screenshot or short screen recording, if you are comfortable sharing one.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information.

Saved progress

Tabiya saves the active run and progress locally. It does not provide account-based or cross-device sync. If Continue Run is missing or a saved run does not resume correctly, close and reopen the App and confirm that you are using the latest version. Do not delete the App as a first troubleshooting step, because doing so may remove local progress.

Settings includes separate controls to delete the current saved run, reset career statistics, and reset tutorial and discovery data. These actions are destructive and cannot be undone from within Tabiya.

Sound or music

Open Settings in Tabiya and confirm that Sound FX and Music are enabled. Also check the device volume and whether audio is being routed to headphones, speakers, or another device.

Daily Reminder

The reminder is optional and scheduled locally for 11:00 a.m. To use it, turn on Settings > Daily Reminder and allow notifications when iOS asks. If it does not appear, open iOS Settings > Notifications > Tabiya and confirm that notifications are allowed. Tabiya does not use remote push notifications.

Local Run Data and bug reports

Local Run Data is optional and off by default. When enabled, it starts with the next run and remains on your device. If support asks for an export, use Settings > Local Run Data > Prepare Export > Share Run Data. Review the destination before sending. You can delete the local records afterward without deleting your save or career progress.
