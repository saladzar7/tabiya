Rank Nine Support

Current as of November 11, 2026

Need help with Rank Nine? Email tabiya.chess@gmail.com. Rank Nine does not require an account, so there is no password or username to include.

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • the App version shown at the bottom of Rank Nine’s Settings screen;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the problem; and
  • a screenshot or short screen recording, if you are comfortable sharing one.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information.

Saved progress

Rank Nine saves the active run and progress locally. It does not provide account-based or cross-device sync. If Continue Climb (called Continue Run in version 1) is missing or a saved run does not resume correctly, close and reopen the App and confirm that you are using the latest version. Do not delete the App as a first troubleshooting step, because doing so may remove local progress.

Settings includes separate controls to delete the current saved run, reset career statistics, and reset tutorial and discovery data. These actions are destructive and cannot be undone from within Rank Nine.

Music and sound effects

Rank Nine includes one main theme used throughout the App, plus separate sound effects. Open Settings and confirm that Music and Sound FX are enabled. They have separate controls. Also check the device volume and whether audio is being routed to headphones, speakers, or another device.

Daily Gauntlet and reminders in version 2.0

Version 2.0 focuses on regular Climb. Daily Gauntlet and its reminder are temporarily unavailable, so the Daily button and reminder setting do not appear. Existing Daily progress and your reminder preference are retained on your device. Updating stops previously scheduled Daily reminders; changing iOS notification permission will not make the mode available in version 2.0.

If you are still using version 1, its optional reminder runs locally at 11:00 a.m. You can turn it off in the older App’s Settings > Daily Reminder or change permission in iOS Settings > Notifications > Rank Nine. Rank Nine does not use remote push notifications.

Local Run Data and bug reports

Local Run Data is optional and off by default. When enabled, it starts with the next run and remains on your device. If support asks for an export, use Settings > Local Run Data > Prepare Export > Share Run Data. Review the destination before sending. You can delete the local records afterward without deleting your save or career progress.
